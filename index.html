From branch to ornament

  1. Walnut branch

    Every piece starts as a length of real walnut branch.

  2. Sliced

    We cut it into rounds and leave the bark edge on.

  3. Sanded

    Each face is sanded smooth, so the grain and growth rings show.

  4. Laser-engraved

    The laser burns the design into the wood, one pass at a time.

  5. Finished

    Strung on twine and ready to hang.

Made by students, by hand.

Etched in Oak is a small studio run by University of Cincinnati students. We slice, sand, and engrave every piece ourselves at UC's 1819 Innovation Hub.

Because each one begins as a real branch, your ornament carries its own rings, its own color, and its own edge of bark.
